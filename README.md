Cricket AI — T20 World Cup Intelligence & Simulation Hub
An Interactive Platform for Cricket Analytics, AI-Assisted Match Simulation, Tournament Intelligence & Performance Visualization

Cricket AI is a modern, interactive cricket intelligence platform designed to bring together match simulation, player performance analytics, tournament forecasting, and strategic decision-making in a unified digital experience. Focused on the T20 World Cup format, the platform combines an immersive cricket interface with data-driven analysis, customizable match scenarios, and interactive visualizations.

The project explores how artificial intelligence, simulation systems, sports analytics, and modern web technologies can be applied to cricket to help users examine match situations, understand player contributions, evaluate team performance, and explore possible tournament outcomes.

Live Demo: https://cricket-ai-t20-world-cup-intelligence-simulation.ai.studio/

📌 Project Overview

Cricket AI aims to transform traditional cricket statistics into an interactive analytical experience. Instead of presenting scores and statistics in isolation, the platform brings together match scenarios, player performance, team comparisons, venue intelligence, and tournament progression.

Designed for cricket enthusiasts, developers, sports analytics researchers, and data science learners, the platform provides an environment for exploring the relationship between match conditions, player contributions, tactical decisions, and potential outcomes.

The system is organized around several interconnected capabilities:

Match simulation and innings progression
Interactive scoreboards and match-centre experiences
Tournament prediction and qualification scenarios
Player and team performance analytics
Ball-by-ball commentary and event visualization
Decision Review System (DRS) analysis
Stadium, pitch, and environmental intelligence
Shot maps, wagon wheels, and trajectory visualization
Player battles, team rivalries, and comparative analysis
🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objective is to explore the application of AI-assisted analysis, statistical modelling, and interactive software engineering within the domain of T20 cricket.

Key objectives include:

🚀 Core Features
1. 🎮 Interactive T20 Match Simulator

The match simulation environment allows users to explore customized cricket scenarios through an interactive interface.

Capabilities include:

Team selection and match configuration
Playing XI selection and lineup customization
Toss decisions and innings configuration
Configurable match formats and over limits
Venue-based match scenarios
Ball-by-ball match progression
Batting, bowling, and innings statistics
Target tracking and run-chase calculations
Wicket, boundary, and milestone events
Match summaries and result visualization

The simulator is designed to make match progression easier to follow while presenting the essential statistics associated with each innings.

2. 📺 Broadcast-Inspired Match Centre

The match centre focuses on presenting cricket information through a familiar live-broadcast-style interface.

Scoreboard elements include:

Total runs and wickets
Overs completed and balls remaining
Current run rate and required run rate
Target and runs required
Batter runs and balls faced
Bowler overs, runs conceded, and wickets
Partnerships and fall of wickets
Innings summaries and match status
Ball-by-ball delivery history

The intended design emphasizes clarity, visual hierarchy, and synchronized match information.

Where supported by the simulation engine, scoreboard values should update automatically as the match progresses.

3. 🏆 T20 World Cup Tournament Intelligence

The tournament module provides an interactive environment for exploring competition structures and possible tournament outcomes.

Analytical areas include:

Participating-team comparisons
Group and league-stage standings
Points-table visualization
Wins, losses, ties, and no-results
Net run rate and qualification scenarios
Potential semifinal and final matchups
Knockout-stage progression
Hypothetical tournament simulations

🔬 Testing & Validation

Reliable cricket analytics requires consistent data and rule-based validation.

Recommended validation areas include:

Score totals against individual delivery records
Batter runs against batting scorecards
Bowler figures against legal deliveries and credited dismissals
Extras and penalty-run calculations
Over and innings progression
Target and required-run calculations
Match results and tie conditions
Tournament points and net run rate
DRS decision scenarios
Data consistency across dashboards and visualizations

Predictive models should additionally be evaluated on suitable historical datasets, with clear separation between training and evaluation data.
